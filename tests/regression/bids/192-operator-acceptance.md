# Issue 192: live operator acceptance script (manual, non-CI)

Use a **sandbox/test opportunity and bid** with non-sensitive source files. Do not submit a live procurement response during UI signoff. Automated tests use deterministic fixtures and no paid AI or live procurement access.

1. Open an opportunity, inspect the original solicitation and understanding, and select **Start bid**. Confirm that **Continue preparing your bid** returns to the same saved bid after navigation, browser refresh, and mobile resize.
2. In a bid with a pending or failed original, confirm the top action names the specific filename and links to **Source snapshot**. The source retrieval control must remain a deliberate user action. Revisit after the server reports stored files; do not check off missing evidence.
3. Use a bid with an amended snapshot and partial understanding. Confirm the snapshot/understanding blockers appear, the paid understanding refresh asks for explicit confirmation, and **Reconcile** is disabled until authoritative evidence is complete and all listed originals have been inspected and checked. Record the displayed reviewed-original count before and after checking each box.
4. Reconcile a test bid with retained Technical response text. Confirm that text and previous source versions remain intact, that only sections needing review are flagged, and that **Technical response** requires its current-source review checkbox and **Save section** while an independent **Pricing response** remains editable. After the save succeeds and the page refreshes, confirm the section-specific blocker clears.
5. Populate a test bid with an unknown-mandatory requirement, incomplete mandatory item, an empty section, a vendor-specific claim requiring factual verification, and an unconfirmed original form. Check that the guided panel groups these distinct final-review issues and links each to the correct requirement/section/form control. Confirm there is no automatic paid drafting or forced rewrite of manual edits.
6. On mobile (a narrow phone viewport) and desktop, use the step navigation by keyboard and touch. Confirm no horizontal page scroll, focus-visible links, readable status names in addition to color, and the ability to return to expert pages without losing saved evidence.
7. Attempt human approval with outstanding final-review blockers: approval must remain disabled and the guided panel must not claim the package is ready. Resolve the test blockers, confirm and save original forms, explicitly approve the exact current package, then amend a source or saved response and confirm previous approval becomes stale.
8. Open the authoritative **external** portal link from final review, but **do not actually submit** the test bid. Confirm that govTract says it cannot submit, does not record a submission receipt, and does not treat an enabled Draft with AI button as final readiness. For an actual bid, the human operator must follow the portal instructions and retain the portal's real receipt separately.

For each check, capture the opportunity ID, bid ID, snapshot fingerprint, expected/actual status, control link, and screenshots or redacted error text. Production signoff requires the full issue acceptance criteria, not just this script.
